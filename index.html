Project Summary & Objectives

This proposal details the comprehensive scope of work to permanently remediate water intrusion along the exterior garage foundation wall at 451 Hilltop Lane. Ground water will be captured at the source below the footing grade, sealed from penetrating the masonry foundation, and routed via a continuous French drain and dedicated discharge run to a safe drainage outlet away from the structure.
2

Comprehensive Scope of Work

Phase 1 20 Linear Feet

Foundation Excavation & Subgrade Prep

  • • Subgrade Excavation: Carefully excavate a continuous 14"–18" wide trench along the full 20-foot exterior garage wall, digging down below the foundation/footing grade to intercept groundwater before it reaches the interior slab level.
  • • Slope & Trench Bedding: Grade the bottom of the trench with a minimum 1% downward slope pitching forward towards the front of the home to ensure positive gravity drainage.
  • • Wall Surface Preparation: Wire brush, clean, and scrape the exposed foundation wall to remove all native clay, dirt, and loose material in preparation for masonry waterproofing.
Phase 2 Hydrostatic Defense

Dual-Stage Waterproofing & Barrier

  • • Masonry Waterproofing: Apply two (2) heavy, continuous coats of commercial-grade masonry waterproofer directly across the exposed foundation wall below grade to seal pores and withstand hydrostatic head pressure.
  • • Heavy-Duty Vapor & Deflector Barrier: Install a heavy-duty moisture barrier over the cured waterproofing coating, extending from just above finished grade down to the base of the footing. This barrier provides a continuous slip-sheet that deflects downward groundwater directly into the drainage bed while protecting the masonry coating from backfill abrasion.
Phase 3 20 Linear Feet

Subsurface French Drain Installation

  • • Filter Envelope: Line the entire 20-foot excavated trench with commercial drainage filter fabric, wrapping the aggregate bed completely to prevent soil intrusion.
  • • Aggregate Bed & Collection Pipe: Place an initial bedding layer of clean drainage aggregate. Lay 20 ft of subsurface perforated collection pipe with filtration sleeve, positioned to capture rising groundwater.
  • • Aggregate Encapsulation: Backfill around and over the collection pipe with remaining clean drainage stone, creating a high-capacity encapsulated drainage column.
  • • Enclosure & Backfill: Fold filter fabric tightly with overlapping joints; backfill with native soil and rough grade with positive fall away from garage.
Phase 4 80 Linear Feet

Solid Discharge Run & Camouflage

  • • Solid Discharge Connection: Transition from the 20' collection line via a dedicated adapter to an 80' continuous run of solid, non-perforated corrugated discharge piping. This solid conveyance line prevents unwanted leaching along the foundation perimeter and ensures captured water is routed directly to the rear outlet.
  • • Surface Installation & Anchoring: Preserves tree roots and grade per homeowner preference; securely anchored along the natural contour of the property.
  • • Landscape Camouflage: Concealed using natural organic ground cover to blend seamlessly into the existing surroundings.
  • • High-Flow Outlet: Terminated at the rear left with a protected drainage emitter for unobstructed daylight discharge.
