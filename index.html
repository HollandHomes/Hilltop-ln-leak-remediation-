Project Summary & Objectives

This proposal details the comprehensive scope of work to permanently remediate water intrusion along the exterior garage foundation wall at 451 Hilltop Lane. Ground water will be captured at the source below the footing grade, sealed from penetrating the masonry foundation, and routed via a continuous French drain and dedicated discharge run to a safe drainage outlet away from the structure.
2

Comprehensive Scope of Work

Phase 1 20 Linear Feet

Foundation Excavation & Subgrade Prep

  • • Subgrade Excavation: Carefully excavate a continuous 14"–18" wide trench along the full 20-foot exterior garage wall, digging down below the foundation/footing grade to intercept groundwater before it reaches the interior slab level.
  • • Slope & Trench Bedding: Grade the bottom of the trench with a minimum 1% downward slope pitching forward towards the front of the home to ensure positive gravity drainage.
  • • Wall Surface Preparation: Wire brush, clean, and scrape the exposed foundation wall to remove all native clay, dirt, and loose material in preparation for masonry waterproofing.
Phase 2 Hydrostatic Defense

Dual-Stage Waterproofing & Barrier

  • • Masonry Waterproofing: Apply two (2) heavy, continuous coats of commercial-grade DRYLOK Extreme Masonry Waterproofer directly across the exposed concrete/block foundation wall below grade to seal pores and withstand hydrostatic head pressure.
  • • Heavy-Duty Vapor & Deflector Barrier: Install a heavy-duty 6-mil/10-mil polyethylene barrier over the cured Drylok coating, extending from just above finished grade down to the base of the footing. This barrier provides a continuous slip-sheet that deflects downward groundwater directly into the gravel drainage bed while protecting the masonry coating from backfill abrasion.
Phase 3 20 Linear Feet

Subsurface French Drain Installation

  • • Geotextile Envelope: Line the entire 20-foot excavated trench with commercial non-woven geotextile drainage fabric, wrapping the stone bed completely.
  • • Gravel Bed & Perforated Pipe: Place an initial 2"–3" bedding layer of clean #57 washed stone (1/3 yd³ system volume). Lay 20 ft of 4" corrugated perforated pipe with filter sock, perforations positioned downward to capture rising groundwater.
  • • Rock Encapsulation: Backfill around and over pipe with remaining clean washed rock, creating an encapsulated gravel column.
  • • Fabric Enclosure & Backfill: Fold fabric tightly with overlapping joints; backfill with native soil and rough grade with positive fall away from garage.
Phase 4 80 Linear Feet

Solid Discharge Run & Camouflage

  • • Watertight PVC Discharge Connection: Transition from 20' perforated line via dedicated transition adapter to an 80' continuous run of rigid 4-inch PVC Sewer & Drain pipe (eight 10-foot sticks, solvent-welded with PVC primer & cement). Unlike flexible corrugated pipe with slitted seams that can allow premature leaching back into the lawn or foundation perimeter, solid rigid PVC is 100% watertight and impermeable, ensuring zero unwanted leaching and guaranteeing captured water is discharged solely at the rear outlet.
  • • Surface Installation & Anchoring: Preserves tree roots and grade per homeowner preference; pinned along the contour with 6" steel landscape U-pins.
  • • Landscape Camouflage: Concealed using premium dark brown shredded bark mulch, pine straw, and native ground cover.
  • • High-Flow Outlet: Terminated at the rear left with an animal guard / pop-up emitter for unobstructed daylight discharge.
